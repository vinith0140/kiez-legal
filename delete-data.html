Kiez – German Exam Prep

Kiez has no accounts. Most of your information is stored only on your phone, and you can delete it yourself at any time.

On your phone

  1. Open Kiez and go to Me.
  2. Tap Start over and confirm with Delete everything.

This deletes your progress, scores, test history, mistakes, words, missions and settings. Uninstalling the app deletes them too.

Information we hold

To delete the information we hold about your use of Kiez (your record of AI checks and any problem reports you sent), email vinith0140@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Kiez data. If you bought an Exam Pass, include the order number from your Google Play receipt (it starts with "GPA."), so we can find your record. We will delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What may be kept

  • Purchase records. These are kept by Google Play under its own policies; we may keep a record of what was bought, as needed for billing, refunds and legal reasons.
  • Your Exam Pass. Deleting your data does not remove a pass you bought; you can restore it with Restore purchases.

Good to know

  • Deleting your data does not refund purchases. For refunds, contact Google Play.
  • Recordings and texts you send for an AI check are never kept, so there is nothing to delete.
