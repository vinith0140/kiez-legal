Privacy Policy

Kiez – German Exam Prep · Effective November 23, 2026

Kiez is an independent study app that helps you prepare for the German exams Goethe-Zertifikat A1, A2 and B1 and telc Deutsch A1. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. We keep it to what the app needs.

Information we collect

  • No account. You use Kiez without signing up. Your progress (scores, test history, mistakes, words, missions, streak, exam date, goal and settings) is stored only on your phone.
  • Purchases. Whether you bought an Exam Pass. Payments are handled by Google Play, and RevenueCat confirms your purchase to the app and our server; we never see your card details.
  • Your voice and your texts, only when you use the AI features. When you record a speaking answer, talk with the AI examiner or with Jens, ask for a writing check, or type a question in “Ask a doubt”, your recording or text is sent to an AI service (OpenAI) to check or answer it. We do not keep your recordings or texts. We keep the AI's answer to a writing check, a question or a coach report for up to 15 minutes, so that the same request sent again (for example after a lost connection) is not counted twice; then it is deleted. OpenAI may keep what it receives for up to 30 days to detect abuse, then deletes it; it does not use it to train its models. Your earlier “Ask a doubt” chats stay only on your phone; with a new question, the app sends the last few questions and answers of that chat so the follow-up makes sense. For the AI coach report, a summary of your test results is sent in the same way.
  • A random app ID. The app creates a random ID that is not linked to your name or email. It is used to count your AI checks and to prevent misuse.
  • Problem reports. If you report a mistake in a question: the question and the note you write.
  • Technical information. Like any app, basic technical data (such as your IP address and app version) is processed by our hosting providers to deliver the app and its audio. We do not use advertising IDs.

How we use it

  • To run the app and play its audio and explanations in your help language.
  • To unlock what you bought.
  • To check your speaking and writing, answer your “Ask a doubt” questions and create your coach report.
  • To fix questions and answers you report.
  • To keep the service safe and prevent misuse.

What we don't do

  • No ads.
  • We don't sell your information.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't share your information for advertising.

Service providers

We use trusted service providers only to run the app: Google Play and RevenueCat (purchases), Cloudflare (hosting, our server and the download of audio) and OpenAI (checking speaking and writing, answering “Ask a doubt” questions, the coach report and the examiner's voice). They process information on our behalf and under their own security and privacy terms. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Security and storage

Information is sent encrypted (HTTPS) and processed with established cloud providers, which may be in the United States, the European Union or other countries. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no system is perfectly secure.

How long we keep it

  • Data on your phone stays until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.
  • Recordings, texts and questions you send to the AI are not kept by us after the answer; the AI's answer is kept up to 15 minutes (see above). OpenAI may keep them up to 30 days to detect abuse.
  • A record of your purchases and of how many AI checks you used is kept as needed for billing, refunds and misuse prevention.
  • Problem reports are kept for up to 6 months.

Your choices

  • Delete your progress any time in the app: Me → Start over.
  • You can use Kiez without the microphone; the AI speaking features are optional.
  • Ask us to delete the information we hold: see how to delete your data.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or ask any privacy question, by email (below).

Children

Kiez is made for teenagers and adults preparing for a German exam. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its date. Important changes will also be shown in the app.
